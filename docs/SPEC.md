# Stakes — product & technical spec

Stakes is a weekly accountability app between friends. Each person plans their tasks for the week, friends review each other every Friday, and a monthly score decides who owes whom. It is a web app (React + Vite + TypeScript) that iPhone users add to their Home Screen. The backend is Firebase on the free Spark plan: Auth (email/password) and Cloud Firestore. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and sends reminder push notifications from a scheduled GitHub Action.

This file is the contract between all modules. If code and spec disagree, fix one of them deliberately. Don't let them drift.

## 1. Rules of the game

### Spaces
- A **space** is a group of 2–8 friends who rate each other.
- A user can be in many spaces.
- The creator writes the stakes as free text, which the app only displays:
  - **reward**: what the others owe a member who hits the bar, e.g. "Dinner on me".
  - **penalty**: what a member who misses owes the others, e.g. "You buy coffee for a week".
- **threshold** = 90 (percent). **latePenalty** = 5 points.
- **timezone** is set to the creator's IANA zone at creation. Every deadline in the space uses this zone. **utcOffsetMin** is that zone's UTC offset at creation (minutes, −720…840). Both are immutable; the rules anchor every week's timeline to the offset (see Week timeline).
- Friends join by invite code (8 characters, alphabet `ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789`, shown as `XXXX-XXXX`) or by an invite link `<app-url>?join=XXXXXXXX`. Anyone with the code can see the invite (space name, stakes, who invited them) before signing up.
- The **owner** (`ownerId`, the creator at first) can remove another member and reset the invite code: the space gets a new code, its invite moves to it, and the old code stops working at once. When the owner leaves and others remain, ownership passes in the same write to the remaining member who joined first.
- Leaving or being removed must record `leftAt[uid]` (the rules refuse a leave or removal without it). Weeks that had locked by then keep the leaver; see Participation.
- **Stakes per month.** Any member can edit the reward and penalty, but each month's stakes freeze once that month's first task lists lock (the first member to open the app after that writes `months/{monthId}`). An edit then only changes months that aren't frozen yet. Before the freeze (e.g. a typo right after creating the space) an edit still applies to the current month. Verdicts and the verdict push quote the frozen stakes, falling back to the space's text for a month that was never frozen. *Superseded by §9.7 (batch 2): only the owner edits these house stakes, an edit counts at once for the month in progress, and a month's stakes are fixed when it is complete; months before October 2026 frozen at their first lock keep what they froze, while the previous app's early freeze of October 2026 (or a later month) doesn't bind and is replaced when that month completes.*

### Week timeline (space timezone)
A week is identified by the date of its Monday: `WeekId = 'YYYY-MM-DD'`. All boundaries are exclusive instants:

| Instant | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `startAt` | Monday 00:00 |
| `lockAt` | Tuesday 00:00, so "locks Monday at midnight". The task list can be edited any time before this, including the week before. |
| `reviewOpenAt` | Friday 00:00. The review window opens. |
| `deadlineAt` | Saturday 00:00, so "reviews due Friday at midnight". |

Phases: `planning` (now < lockAt) → `active` (lockAt ≤ now < reviewOpenAt) → `review` (reviewOpenAt ≤ now < deadlineAt) → `closed`.

Once a week doc exists, its stored timestamps are the week's timeline: the rules judge every write by them, so the screens, the data layer's own checks and the reminders job use them too. The computed bounds are only used before the doc exists.

The rules keep the stored timeline honest: `startAt` must be within 1h of Monday 00:00 UTC − `utcOffsetMin` (the anchor; the hour covers DST and a space created in the other season), and every later bound within 1h of whole days after `startAt` and within 1h of the same whole days after the anchor, so the two hours of slack can't add up. Spaces created before `utcOffsetMin` existed fall back to "within 14h of Monday 00:00 UTC" until the reminders job backfills their offset (it also corrects an offset more than 1h off the zone's). If the stored bounds still differ from the zone's by more than an hour, `WeekData.boundsMismatch` is true and the screens should say so; the stored times still rule.

The UI shows **this week** and **next week**. Planning next week is allowed at any time.

### Participation
- Everyone who is a space member before a week's `lockAt` takes part in it, whether or not they open it. Whenever any member opens the app (the spaces list, a space or a week), the client makes sure this week (until it locks) and next week exist and hold every current member. A week nobody opens before `lockAt` has no doc and no participants.
- Someone who joins a space after a week's `lockAt` starts with the next week (`space.joinedAt[uid] ≥ lockAt`; `joinedAfterLock()` in the domain tells this apart from a member who simply has no tasks).
- A participant with **zero tasks** at lock scores **0%** that week ("you committed to nothing").
- **The lock is a commitment.** "Your list locks Monday at midnight; from then on that week counts, even if you leave."
  - Leaving the space, deleting your account or being removed by the owner *before* a week's `lockAt` takes you out of that week: this week while it is still planning, and next week.
  - From `lockAt` on it doesn't: you stay in `week.members`. Your friends still review you (and lose points if they don't), your score counts toward that month (in the month's results you are `former` and still owe or are owed), you are charged for the reviews you no longer can write, and the reveal still waits for you. Since you can't submit any more, that means the deadline, unless you had already submitted before leaving. Leaving never reveals a week early, never hides a bad week, and never removes you from anyone's list.
  - Who counts for a week is `membersAt(space, lockAt)`: the current members plus anyone with `leftAt ≥ lockAt`, except a current member who left before `lockAt` and rejoined after it (rejoining never rewrites a locked week). A member who never left counts whatever their `joinedAt` says.

### During the week
- Each participant can tick their own tasks as done ("self check-offs") until `deadlineAt`. Everyone in the space can see them. A task with steps (§6.1) is ticked step by step.
- After the lock and before the review window opens (`lockAt ≤ now < reviewOpenAt`), a participant can **add** tasks to their list, one at a time (§6.2). Added tasks count like the rest and are reviewed on Friday. They can't be edited or removed, and everyone sees when they were added. Nothing already on the list can change after the lock.

### Friday review
- Every participant reviews every other participant who has ≥1 task.
- Each task gets a mark: **Done = 1**, **Half = 0.5**, **Not done = 0**.
- Reviews are written once, on **Submit**, with all reviewees in a single batch. They are **final**: no edits after submitting.
- Reviews can only be created inside the review window.
- Only current space members can submit.
- **Blind**: you can't see reviews written by others, about you or anyone else, until they are revealed. Reveal happens when every week member has submitted (`submittedBy ⊇ members`) or at `deadlineAt`, whichever comes first. A member who left after lock is still a week member, so the reveal waits for them; as they can't submit any more, the deadline reveals the week (see Participation). You can always see your own submitted reviews. The data layer never publishes a revealed week with only your own reviews: it holds the previous state until everyone's have loaded.

### Weekly score (per participant M with n tasks)
- If n = 0, the score is 0.
- If at least one other participant submitted a review of M, each task's mark is the average of the submitted reviewers' marks for that task. A missing mark for a task counts as 0.
- If nobody reviewed M (all reviewers missed the deadline, or M is alone in the space), each task's mark is M's self check-off: done = 1, else 0. For a task with steps it is the share of steps ticked (2 of 4 = 0.5).
- `score = 100 × Σ marks / n`, a number from 0 to 100.
- Before reveal, the UI may show a **provisional** score computed from self check-offs, clearly labelled.

### Missed reviews
After `deadlineAt`, a participant R **missed** a review of M if M had ≥1 task, M still counts for that week (see Participation) and no review R→M exists. Each miss costs R `latePenalty` (5) points off their own monthly score. M is not punished; M's tasks fall back to M's self check-offs only if *no one* reviewed M.

### Monthly score
- A week belongs to the month of its **Friday**: `MonthId = 'YYYY-MM'` of the Friday's date (`monthIdForWeek`), whatever the week's times.
- A member's month average is the mean of their scores in **closed** weeks of that month in which they participated.
- `finalScore = max(0, average − latePenalty × missedReviewsInMonth)`. If there is no closed participating week, it is `null`. `average` and `finalScore` are rounded to 1e-9, so a month that averages exactly 90 reads as exactly 90 everywhere.
- The month's roster is everyone who took part in one of its weeks plus the current members; someone who has left since is marked `former`, keeps their real scores for the weeks that had locked before they left, and still owes or is owed for it. Each result lists its `others`: everyone else who took part that month. Members who joined later aren't in anyone's `others`.
- A month is **complete** when `now ≥ deadlineAt` of its last week. Until then the UI shows the score so far.
- When complete: `finalScore ≥ threshold` means the month's `others` owe M the reward. Otherwise M owes them the penalty. The reward and penalty are the month's frozen stakes (see Spaces; since batch 2, the terms in force between each pair when the month was complete, §9.7). With no `others`, nothing is owed.
- Settlements are honor-system toggles ("Settled") stored per month per member, current or former. **No money moves through the app.**

## 2. Firestore data model

Timestamps are Firestore `Timestamp` in storage and epoch milliseconds (`number`) in TypeScript domain types. The data layer converts between them.

```
users/{uid}                                  { name, color, photo?, createdAt }   # photo: inline JPEG data URL (§7.3)
users/{uid}/push/{subId}                     { endpoint, keys: { p256dh, auth }, createdAt, ua }
users/{uid}/money/state                      { currency, baseline: number|null, baselineAt: ts|null, updatedAt }   # private (§6.3)
users/{uid}/earnings/{spaceId}_{weekId}_{taskId}  { spaceId, weekId, taskId, title, amount, earnedAt: ts|null, updatedAt }  # private
users/{uid}/moves/{moveId}                   { kind: 'add'|'spend', amount, note, at, goal?: true }                 # private
users/{uid}/goals/{goalId}                   { name, cost, order, createdAt }                                       # private
invites/{code}                               { spaceId, spaceName, reward, penalty, inviterName, createdBy, createdAt }
spaces/{spaceId}                             { name, reward, penalty, ownerId, memberIds[], joinedAt{uid: ts}, leftAt?{uid: ts},
                                               timezone, utcOffsetMin, inviteCode, threshold: 90, latePenalty: 5, createdAt }
spaces/{spaceId}/joins/{uid}                 { code, createdAt }            # proof of invite, written in the join batch
spaces/{spaceId}/weeks/{weekId}              { startAt, lockAt, reviewOpenAt, deadlineAt, members[], submittedBy[] }
spaces/{spaceId}/weeks/{weekId}/plans/{uid}     { tasks: [{ id, title, subs?: ["<subId>|<title>", …] }], added?: { [taskId]: ts }, updatedAt }
spaces/{spaceId}/weeks/{weekId}/progress/{uid}  { done: { [taskId]: boolean }, updatedAt }
spaces/{spaceId}/weeks/{weekId}/reviews/{reviewerId}_{revieweeId}
                                             { reviewerId, revieweeId, marks: { [taskId]: 0|0.5|1 }, submittedAt }
spaces/{spaceId}/months/{monthId}            { reward, penalty, createdAt }  # the month's frozen (house) stakes; since batch 2 written once the month is complete (§9.7), overwriting the previous app's early freeze from 2026-10 on
spaces/{spaceId}/settlements/{monthId}_{uid} { monthId, uid, settled, settledBy, settledAt }
public/config                                { vapidPublicKey }              # written by the reminders job (admin)
_private/*                                   admin only (VAPID private key, sent-notification log)
```

Limits: space name 1–40 chars; reward/penalty 1–120 chars; user name 1–40 chars; ≤25 tasks per plan; task title 1–120 chars; ≤8 steps per task, step title 1–120 chars on one line; task ids `[A-Za-z0-9_-]{1,64}` (also as review mark keys and check-off keys); ≤8 members per space; profile color `#RRGGBB`; profile photo ≤ 60,000 characters.

### Security rules (`firestore.rules`), what must hold
- `users/{uid}`: any signed-in user can `get` a profile; `list` is denied, so nobody can enumerate users. Only the owner can write, with field validation; `color` must be `#RRGGBB` (it lands in CSS). `photo` is optional: a string of at most 60,000 characters (checked before the pattern) matching `^data:image/jpeg;base64,[A-Za-z0-9+/]+=*$`, so it can never be a link that loads from every viewer's phone. A write without it (older clients, a name change) keeps working.
- `users/{uid}/push/*`: only the owner can read and write. `endpoint` must be on a real push service: `web.push.apple.com`, `fcm.googleapis.com`, `updates.push.services.mozilla.com` or `*.notify.windows.com`. The number of subscriptions is not capped by the rules; the reminders job only uses each user's 5 newest.
- `users/{uid}/money/state`, `earnings/*`, `moves/*`, `goals/*` (§6.3): only the owner can read and write. The profile's `get` doesn't reach them (rules don't cascade) and there is no collection-group access. Exact keys and types:
  - `money/state` (no other id): `currency` matches `^[A-Z]{3}$`; `baseline` null or a number within ±1e9; `baselineAt` null or a timestamp, and `request.time` whenever the baseline changes (otherwise unchanged); `updatedAt == request.time`.
  - `earnings/{eid}`: `eid == spaceId + '_' + weekId + '_' + taskId`, `spaceId` 1–128 characters, `weekId` matches `^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$`, `taskId` like a task id, `title` 1–120 characters, `amount` 0…1e9, `earnedAt` null, `request.time` or (on update) unchanged, `updatedAt == request.time`.
  - `moves/*`: create and delete only. `kind` is `add` or `spend`, `amount` > 0 and ≤ 1e9, `note` 0–80 characters, `at == request.time`; `goal` is optional, and when present it is `true` on a `spend` (a goal marked as bought).
  - `goals/*`: `name` 1–60 characters and not blank, `cost` > 0 and ≤ 1e9, `order` within ±1e6, `createdAt == request.time` on create and unchanged after.
- `invites/{code}`: anyone, signed in or not, can `get` (the code is the secret); `list` is denied. Only a member of `spaceId` can create, with `createdBy == auth.uid`, only under the space's own `inviteCode`, and with `spaceName`, `reward` and `penalty` equal to the space's (checked with `getAfter`, so the space and its invite can be written in one batch). A member can update `spaceName`/`reward`/`penalty` alone, and only to mirror the space. The creator or any space member can delete.
- `spaces/{id}`: only members can read. Create requires `ownerId == auth.uid`, `memberIds == [auth.uid]`, `joinedAt == {auth.uid: request.time}`, `utcOffsetMin` an int in −720…840, threshold 90, latePenalty 5, and validated fields. Allowed updates, and nothing else (timezone, utcOffsetMin, threshold, latePenalty and createdAt never change):
  - a member edits `name`/`reward`/`penalty`;
  - a member leaves: removes self from `memberIds` and `joinedAt` and sets `leftAt[self] = request.time` (required; nothing else in `leftAt` changes); if the leaver is the owner and others remain, `ownerId` must become a remaining member with the earliest `joinedAt`, otherwise `ownerId` stays;
  - the owner removes one other member the same way (`memberIds`, `joinedAt`, and the required `leftAt[them] = request.time`);
  - the owner sets a new, valid `inviteCode` (alone);
  - in a space whose `ownerId` is no longer a member, a member sets `ownerId` (alone) to the earliest-joined member;
  - a non-member joins: only `memberIds` (appending self) and `joinedAt` (adding self = request.time) change, `getAfter(spaces/{id}/joins/{auth.uid}).data.code` is the space's current `inviteCode` and names an invite whose `spaceId == id`, and the member count stays ≤ 8.
- `spaces/{id}/joins/{uid}`: create only by `uid == auth.uid`, and only if `invites/{code}.spaceId == id`. Read by self.
- `weeks/{weekId}`: members can read. A member can create it when:
  - `weekId` is a Monday date;
  - the timeline is anchored to the space: `startAt` within 1h of `weekId` 00:00 UTC − `utcOffsetMin` (within 14h of `weekId` 00:00 UTC for a space without `utcOffsetMin`); `lockAt`, `reviewOpenAt`, `deadlineAt` within 1h of `startAt` + 1, 4, 5 days and, with `utcOffsetMin`, also within 1h of that anchor + 1, 4, 5 days;
  - `submittedBy == []`;
  - `members` holds exactly the space's current members;
  - `request.time < lockAt`, and `startAt < request.time + 8 days` (no far-future weeks).

  Allowed updates, and nothing else:
  - before `lockAt`, a space member appends current space members who are missing from `members` (no duplicates);
  - before `lockAt`, one uid leaves `members` if it is no longer a space member (`getAfter`, so it goes in the same batch as leaving the space); the writer is that uid or the space's owner. From `lockAt` on nobody leaves `members`, so leaving or removal can't take anyone out of a locked week;
  - add self to `submittedBy` while `reviewOpenAt ≤ request.time < deadlineAt`, self ∈ `members` and self is still a space member.
- `plans/{uid}`: members of the space can read. Only the owner writes, while owner ∈ `week.members` and still a space member:
  - before `lockAt`: exactly `{tasks, updatedAt}` (no `added`), `updatedAt == request.time`; `tasks` is a list of ≤ 25 tasks, each exactly `{id, title}` or `{id, title, subs}`: `id` like a task id, `title` 1–120 characters, `subs` 1–8 strings `"<id>|<title>"` (step id like a task id, step title 1–120 characters on one line). Deleting the plan is allowed only before `lockAt`;
  - while `lockAt ≤ request.time < reviewOpenAt`, append only (§6.2): exactly `{tasks, added, updatedAt}`; `tasks` is the stored list unchanged (same order, same content) plus one valid task, ≤ 25 in all (with no plan yet: just that task); `added` gains exactly that task's id, not already in it, with `request.time` and keeps every other entry; `updatedAt == request.time`.

  A plan of 25 tasks with 8 steps each fits the 1000-expression limit (about 890, measured in the emulator); the task check is written for cost (see the comments in `firestore.rules`).
- `progress/{uid}`: members can read. The owner can write while `request.time < deadlineAt` and owner ∈ `week.members`; `done` has ≤ 200 boolean entries and each write adds at most one key, shaped like a task id.
- `reviews/{rid}`:
  - create only, no update or delete;
  - `rid == reviewerId + '_' + revieweeId`; `reviewerId == auth.uid`; reviewer and reviewee ∈ `week.members` and differ;
  - `marks` has ≤ 25 keys shaped like task ids, values 0 / 0.5 / 1;
  - `reviewOpenAt ≤ request.time < deadlineAt`;
  - reviewer ∉ `get(week).submittedBy` (pre-batch state) and ∈ `getAfter(week).submittedBy` (so it is the submit batch, which also requires the reviewer to still be a member);
  - `submittedAt == request.time`.

  Read: the reviewer, or a space member when revealed (`request.time ≥ deadlineAt || week.submittedBy ⊇ week.members`).

  Clients must query in ways the rules can prove. Before reveal: `where('reviewerId','==',uid)`. After reveal: the whole collection.
- `months/{monthId}`: members can read. A member can create it (never update or delete) with exactly `{reward, penalty, createdAt}`, `reward`/`penalty` equal to the space's current ones, `createdAt == request.time`, and `monthId` between the month of `request.time − 3 days` and the month of `request.time + 5 days` (the month of the current week's Friday in any zone). Batch 2 replaces the month window: only once the month is complete, and an early freeze of a month from 2026-10 on can be overwritten once, at completion (§9.7).
- `settlements/*`: members can read and write, with field validation; `uid` is a current member or one in `leftAt`.
- `public/config`: signed-in users can read; nobody can write. `_private/**`: no client access.

### Data layer (`src/data/api.ts`), behaviour the screens rely on
- `createSpace` writes the space and its invite in one batch. It first checks the server answers (8 s); offline it fails at once with the offline message instead of queueing, and a second identical call while the first is in flight returns the first.
- `lookupInvite` works signed out; `Invite` carries `reward` and `penalty` (empty on invites from before). `updateSpace` keeps the invite's name and stakes in step.
- `leaveSpace`, `deleteMyAccount` and `removeMember` take the user out of the weeks that haven't locked yet (this week while it is planning, next week) and never out of a locked week (see Participation). They record `leftAt`, and leaving hands ownership on. `removeMember(spaceId, uid)` and `resetInviteCode(spaceId) → new code` are owner-only.
- `useMySpaces`, `useSpace` and `useWeek` run `ensureSpace`: this week (until it locks) and next week hold every current member, the current month's stakes are frozen once its first lists have locked (batch 2: once the month is complete, §9.7), and a space whose owner already left (older versions) passes to its earliest-joined member.
- `useMonthStakes(space, monthId)` gives `{ reward, penalty, frozen }`, falling back to the space's text with `frozen: false`. Since batch 2 it also gives the freeze's `createdAt`, and whether a freeze binds is `monthHouse`'s call (§9.7).
- `useWeek(...).data.boundsMismatch` flags a skewed stored timeline. A revealed week is never published with only the user's own reviews.
- After joining or leaving, the cached space, weeks, months and settlements of that space are dropped (`forget`), so nothing stale lingers.
- `sendPasswordReset` resolves for unknown emails too (no account enumeration).
- `updateMyPhoto(photo | null)` sets or deletes (`null`) your `photo`. A value the rules would refuse is refused before writing, so it never shows from the local cache; failures come back as plain messages. `toProfile` passes `photo` through only when it is an inline JPEG of the allowed size, so a stored link never reaches an `<img>`.
- `savePlan` takes tasks with `subtasks` (≤ 8; task and step ids unique across the list; blank steps dropped, titles made one line). After the save, in the background, that week's earnings follow the list: a renamed task renames its earning, a deleted task deletes it. A failure there never fails the save; the next save fixes it.
- `addTaskAfterLock(space, weekId, { id?, title, subtasks })` checks the window, membership and the 25-task cap with its own messages, then appends in a transaction that writes exactly what the rules allow (§6.2). If the plan already holds a task with that id, an earlier attempt went through (a lost answer, a retried transaction, the user trying again with the same `id`), and it resolves without writing. It resolves with the task; its `addedAt` is the device's estimate until the plan's snapshot brings the server time.
- `setTaskDone(space, weekId, id, done)` takes a task id or a step id. When the device has the plan, the check-offs and the earning, the check-off and the earning's `earnedAt` are one batch; otherwise the check-off is saved and the earning is settled from the server afterwards. Check-offs of one week run one at a time on the device until each one's write is issued (not confirmed, so offline ticks don't queue), so two quick taps decide from each other's ticks. A new tick that would take a progress doc past 200 keys first clears ticks of tasks and steps no longer on the list (deleted before the lock), so a full 25 × 8 list always fits.
- Money hooks (`useMoneyState`, `useEarnings`, `useMoves`, `useGoals`, `useCurrency`) are scoped to the signed-in user; pending server timestamps read as the SDK's estimate, so a fresh write shows at once. Creating `money/state` writes every field, after asking the server when the cache says it's missing (a stale cache must not reset the currency). `deleteMyAccount` also deletes `money/state` and every earning, move and goal, before it leaves the spaces: leaving can't be undone, so a failure there leaves the user in their spaces to try again.

Known limits:
- The rules don't cap how many spaces an account creates.
- A profile with a photo is up to about 60 KB, so opening a full 8-person space the first time downloads up to ~480 KB of profiles (cached after that). Every signed-in user who knows a uid can read that profile's photo, as with names.
- A user's private money docs are unbounded too: the rules don't cap how many earnings, moves and goals one account stores (one earning per revenue task, one move per Add/Remove Money, forever). Overview and Goals listen to the whole `earnings` and `moves` collections, so years of history mean more reads each time the app opens cold (the Spark plan allows 50,000 reads a day for everyone together). Only the owner can read or write them, so a large history costs its owner and the shared quota, nobody's privacy. Paging or yearly rollups would be the fix if it ever matters.
- Steps are checked with one regex over the joined list, which can't count lines within the expression budget: a step whose title hides a newline followed by more step-like text (`"a|x\nb|y"` as one step) passes. Step ids are still always valid; only the writer's own step title is affected.
- The rules don't check that task ids are unique within a plan (the data layer makes new ones with `newTaskId()`). Marks, check-offs and `added` go by id, so the client (`decodeTasks`, also used by the reminders job) keeps only the first task with an id and ignores `added` for an id that repeats: a copy appended after the lock can't count twice, share the original's mark or give it an "Added" badge. A step whose id is also a task id is dropped from its task, which only affects the writer's own list.
- A revenue task's switch is the existence of its earning, so switching it off and on again for a done task earns it anew (now): if it had been earned before the balance was last set, the balance counts it a second time. Un-ticking and re-ticking doesn't (§6.3).
- A determined member can still write a week with a skew of up to an hour, up to 2h on a space created in the other DST season (its stored offset is an hour off), or up to 14h on a space the reminders job hasn't backfilled yet. The client flags it (`boundsMismatch`).
- A retired invite code (reset, or the last member left) can be claimed by another space: anyone who knows it can create a space with that `inviteCode` and its invite, and the old link then previews and joins that space instead. Low severity: it needs the old code, and the preview shows the new space's name and inviter.

## 4. Platform
- **Hosting**: GitHub Pages from the `gh-pages` branch. `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` builds on push to `main` or `claude/**` and publishes `dist/`, including a `.nojekyll` file. The Vite base is `./`.
- **Rules before the app**: the rules are published by hand in the Firebase console, and every push to those branches deploys. A round that changes `firestore.rules` publishes them first, then pushes; or it pushes with `[skip ci]` and runs the Deploy workflow by hand (`workflow_dispatch`) once the rules are live. New rules must keep accepting what old clients write (they keep running for a while after a deploy); a new client against old rules is refused (e.g. steps, money), which is why the rules go first.
- **Bundle** (`vite.config.ts`): vendor chunks `firebase`, `react`, `motion` and `luxon` sit apart from the app chunk, so a typical deploy re-downloads only the app (~70 kB gzip since the second round, plus ~14 kB of CSS). Screens are not route-split: a resumed old client would 404 on lazy chunks removed by the next deploy. `re2js` (only used by Firestore's client-side pipeline regex functions, which the app never calls) is aliased to a stub that throws (`src/stubs/re2js.ts`).
- **Updates**: each build has an id (the commit SHA on CI) that the build writes into `dist/sw.js` and into the page. iOS resumes Home Screen apps without navigating, so on every return to the app (`visibilitychange`, at most every 30 minutes) the client calls `registration.update()`. When the worker in charge reports a different build than the page, the page reloads, unless a text field is focused or a sheet or alert is open; then a banner says "Updated — tap to reload" and the reload happens on tap or on the next idle return. A first install, or a launch that already loaded the new build, does nothing.
- **Navigation**: in-app state stack, with no URL routes. GitHub Pages has no rewrites. Query params are used only for `?join=`, plus `?now=` in dev.
- **Firebase config**: `src/firebase/config.ts`. Until the real web config is pasted in, the app shows a "Not configured yet" screen. With `VITE_EMULATORS=1` it connects to the local emulators (auth 9099, firestore 8080, project `demo-stakes`).
- **Push**: Web Push (VAPID) works on iOS 16.4+ only when the app is installed to the Home Screen.
  - The client subscribes using `public/config.vapidPublicKey` and stores the subscription under `users/{uid}/push`.
  - `.github/workflows/reminders.yml` runs hourly at :17 and on manual dispatch. GitHub runs schedules only from the default branch (`main`), so the file lives there, but it checks out the branch the app deploys from (`claude/task-rating-app-feasibility-abixvp`, or the repository variable `APP_BRANCH`) so the live code sends the reminders. It runs `node scripts/reminders.ts` with the `FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT` secret and exits 0 when the secret is missing.
  - The job creates the VAPID keys in `_private/vapid` if missing and mirrors the public key to `public/config`.
  - It sends each reminder at most once, tracked by docs under `_private/sent/log/{key}`. Copy gives time left relative to now ("locks in 6 hours", "Due in 15 hours"), so it is right in every member's timezone. Once a week doc exists, its stored bounds decide (no "due in" after the stored deadline).
    - Monday 18:00+ local, until the stored lock: you have no tasks for this week and the list locks in N hours. "Nothing planned = 0%" only for members already in the week doc.
    - Wednesday 18:00+ local, until Thursday 00:00 local: "Nothing ticked yet", N tasks to go before Friday, to a participant with tasks who hasn't ticked a task or step on their current list (ticks left by deleted tasks, or a stepped task's own key, don't count).
    - Thursday 18:00+ local, until the stored review opening: "K of N tasks still open", reviews start in H hours, to a participant with a task not done (a task with steps is done once every step is ticked), whether or not they had Wednesday's nudge. Both nudges reach only current members in `week.members`; the job reads just the week doc, the plans of those still waiting with a device, and the progress of those with tasks.
    - Friday 09:00+ local: review your friends, due in N hours. Only current members are reminded, but the friends to rate are everyone counted for the week (`membersAt(space, lockAt)`), including anyone who left after lock (named "a former member" once their profile is gone).
    - Friday 20:00+ local: N hours left, or −5 points per friend.
    - Saturday 09:00+ local: results are in (once the week is revealed).
    - On the 1st of the month at 10:00+ local: last month's verdict, quoting that month's frozen stakes (batch 2: the terms that counted between each pair, §9.11); nothing for a member with nobody else in that month.
  - Expired subscriptions (404/410) are deleted. Only the 5 newest subscriptions per user on an allowed push host are used.
  - Hardening: each push send times out after 10 s (a failed delivery, retried next run), each space has a 60 s budget (then it is left for the next run), and a space whose timezone the runtime doesn't support is skipped and logged. None of these fail the run; only an unexpected error in a space does.
  - It backfills `utcOffsetMin` (from the space's own zone) on spaces created before it existed, and corrects one that is more than 1h off the zone's current offset (whoever creates a space can write any offset).
- **Installing on iOS**: Safari and Home Screen apps have separate storage, so a friend may have to sign in twice and re-enter an invite code. The UI must make the invite code easy to type, as well as offering the link. When the app runs in a Safari tab on iOS, show a dismissible "Add to Home Screen" guide; reminders only work after installing.

## 6. Tasks with steps, tasks added mid-week, money, Overview and Goals

This section is the contract for the second feature round. Where it and the sections above disagree, this one wins.

### 6.1 Tasks with steps (subtasks)
- A task can have up to **8 steps** (`Task.subtasks`, each `{ id, title }`; ids like task ids, from `newTaskId()`). Steps are planned with the task and follow its rules: editable until the lock (the whole task), frozen after it.
- **Check-offs**: each step is ticked on its own (`progress.done[subId]`, one key per write as before). A task with steps has no checkbox of its own: it shows how many steps are done (e.g. a small ring and "2/4") and counts as done once every step is ticked. `progress.done[task.id]` is ignored for a task with steps.
- **Scoring**: reviewers mark the task, not its steps (Done / Half / Not done); they see the steps and which were ticked, as evidence. In the self check-off fallback and the provisional score, a task with steps counts the share of steps ticked (`selfFraction`).
- **Storage**: a task is stored as `{ id, title }` or `{ id, title, subs }` with `subs` a non-empty list of strings `"<subId>|<title>"` (split on the first `|`; titles have no newlines). The data layer converts to and from `Task.subtasks`; `subs` is omitted when there are no steps.
- **Caps**: 25 tasks × 8 steps = 200 check-off keys, the existing cap of a progress doc. The UI stops at 8 steps per task. Ticks left behind by tasks or steps deleted before the lock are cleared when a new tick needs the room (§2, data layer).
- **On screen**: before the week starts (Monday 00:00) steps show as bullets under their task; from then on each has its own check circle and the task shows a ring and "2/4" instead of its own. Your own ring is a button (it sits where people tap to tick) that opens the task. Friends see gray dots and a gray ring. The Overview ring and the provisional score count steps as fractions; "N of M done" lines count whole tasks.

### 6.2 Tasks added after the lock
- While `lockAt ≤ now < reviewOpenAt`, a participant who is still a space member can append **one task per write** (with its steps) to their plan, up to 25 in all. "Thursday at midnight" is the last moment: on Friday everyone reviews the same list.
- The appended task's add time is the server time of the write, stored in the plan's `added` map (`added[taskId] = request.time`; arrays can't hold server timestamps). The data layer exposes it as `Task.addedAt`.
- Nothing else in the plan changes in that write: the existing tasks are exactly as before (same order, same content), `added` gains exactly that one key, and the plan's `updatedAt` is the server time. A participant with no plan at the lock can create one this way (one task). Added tasks can never be edited or removed.
- Before the lock, plans are written as before (`{ tasks, updatedAt }`, no `added`).
- **Shown to everyone**: an added task carries a small "Added Wed" badge (weekday of `addedAt` in the space timezone) on every list and in the review sheet, so friends can judge padding (e.g. an easy task added Thursday night). The add sheet says plainly that the task becomes part of the week, is reviewed Friday, shows the day it was added and can't be edited or removed; its one action is a full-width **Add to This Week** button (no second alert).
- Reviews, reveal and missed reviews are unchanged: someone with no tasks at the lock who adds one becomes a review target like anyone with a task.

### 6.3 Money (private)
Everything here lives under `users/{uid}` and is readable and writable **only by that user**. Friends never see amounts, revenue switches, balances or goals.
- **Currency**: `money/state.currency`, an ISO 4217 code (3 uppercase letters), one of the 29 the picker lists (`CURRENCIES`). Amounts are formatted en-US like the rest of the copy ("$1,250", "€1,250.50", cents only when there are some, "−" for negatives). Amount fields edit them as plain digits ("1250", "1250.50", `editableAmount`): grouped, an added or removed digit ("1,2500", "4,50") or cents typed on a comma keypad ("1,250,50") would read as a decimal comma or not at all. Text a field can't read is never saved: the sheet's Done stays disabled. Until set, the client uses a default from the device locale's region (e.g. en-US → USD, de-DE → EUR, hy-AM → AMD, en-GB → GBP), falling back to USD. All amounts of a user are in that one currency; changing it relabels, it doesn't convert.
- **Revenue tasks**: any of your own tasks (in any week, before or after the lock) can be switched to "Generates revenue" with an amount (≥ 0, 2 decimals, ≤ 1,000,000,000). The switch is the existence of `earnings/{spaceId}_{weekId}_{taskId}`; switching off deletes it. The amount can be changed at any time (plan an estimate, correct it when paid). It is your own bookkeeping, so it is not tied to the lock.
  - `earnedAt` is set (server time) when the task becomes done by your self check-off (a task with steps: its last step), and cleared when it is un-done, unless it is at or before `baselineAt` (`inBaseline`): that money is already in the balance you set, so it stays earned and ticking the task again can't count it twice. Such a task still shows as earned (Overview, Activity, its amount badge). The data layer keeps `earnedAt` in step in `setTaskDone`, when the switch is turned on for a task that is already done, and when the task sheet saves steps that make the task done or not (Monday, before the lock). Friends' marks don't affect money.
  - The data layer keeps `title` in step when a task is renamed before the lock and deletes the earning when the task is deleted before the lock. Leaving a space keeps your earnings.
- **Moves**: money added (`add`) or spent (`spend`) by hand, with an optional note (≤ 80 chars). A move can be deleted (undo), not edited.
- **Balance** = `baseline` (0 if unset) + Σ moves with `at > baselineAt` (add +, spend −) + Σ earnings with `earnedAt > baselineAt` (`amount`). With no baseline set, every move and earning counts. "Set balance" (I have X now) writes `baseline = X, baselineAt = now`; what was earned or moved before is assumed to be in X. The balance can go negative (shown in red).
- **Goals**: `{ name, cost, order }`. The balance fills goals in order, top first (a waterfall): goal 1 gets `min(balance, cost₁)`, goal 2 gets what is left, and so on. Each goal shows funded / cost, what is left to go, and an estimate "≈ N weeks at your pace" where pace = net money in over the last 28 days (earnings + adds − spends, not counting goals marked as bought: that money was saved earlier) ÷ 4, only when pace > 0 and N ≤ 104. A funded goal can be marked **Bought**: one batch adds a `spend` move of its cost (note = goal name, `goal: true`) and deletes the goal. The spend comes off the balance like any other. Goals can be reordered, edited and deleted.
- **Rules** (`firestore.rules`): owner only (`isUser(uid)`) for read and write on `money/state`, `earnings/*`, `moves/*`, `goals/*`, with exact keys and types: amounts are numbers within 0…1e9 (moves and costs > 0), strings within their limits, `currency` matches `^[A-Z]{3}$`, `updatedAt`/`at`/`createdAt` are `request.time` when written, `earnedAt` is null or `request.time` (or unchanged), the earnings doc id equals `spaceId + '_' + weekId + '_' + taskId`, and `weekId` matches `^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$`. Moves are create/delete only; a move's optional `goal` is `true` on a spend. `deleteMyAccount` deletes all of it.

### 6.4 Navigation: tab bar
- Signed in, the app is a **tab bar** with three tabs, each its own `NavigationStack` (state kept while switching): **Spaces** (`squareStack`), **Overview** (`chartBar`), **Goals** (`target`). The last tab is remembered on the device. Tapping the selected tab pops its stack to the root, and at the root scrolls it to the top; since the bar hides while a screen is pushed, the scroll is what people meet. The bar slides back in as a pop starts (`useNav().toDepth`), not after it lands.
- iOS 26 look: a floating glass capsule centered above the home indicator, icon over a small label, the selected item on a soft glass pill; it slides away while the keyboard is up and when a screen is pushed on top of a tab's root (like `hidesBottomBarWhenPushed`), so pushed screens keep their own floating toolbars. Sheets cover it. Root screens leave room for it at the bottom.
- A notification or invite link that opens a space switches to the Spaces tab first.

### 6.5 Overview
A root screen, "Overview", across all of your spaces:
- **This week**: one large ring with your tasks done across every space this week (steps count as fractions), "5 of 8 tasks done", then one row per space with its mini score ring and what's next ("Review due Fri", "Locks tonight"). The ring shows the revealed score ("Score"), or from Monday 00:00 the provisional one from your check-offs ("Provisional", as on the Space screen) once you have tasks; "So far" is kept for month-to-date scores.
- **Money**: earned this month (large, in your currency) with this week, still pending (revenue tasks not done yet in this week and next week) and all time (from 100,000 these three go compact, "$123K", "֏5.3M", never cut off; VoiceOver reads the exact amount); a bar chart of earnings in the last 8 weeks (this week highlighted); the top 3 earning tasks of the month (tasks without an amount are left out). Money's "this week" is the device's week, since money spans spaces. A Goals row switches to the Goals tab. With no revenue tasks yet, a short explanation of how to mark a task as revenue (in Spaces, ⓘ next to a task).
- **This month**: per space, the month-to-date score against 90 ("On track", or "4 points short"; once the month is complete "Cleared the bar" or "Missed by 4 points"). Red is kept for a finished month that missed.
- Weeks and months are the spaces' own (a week is identified by its Monday; money is grouped by the task's `weekId`, a month by `monthIdForWeek`).

### 6.6 Goals
A root screen, "Goals":
- **Balance** card: the balance, large; under it "+ $X earned from tasks this month"; two clear buttons, **Add Money** and **Remove Money** (a spend, with an optional "What for?" note); a menu with **Set Balance** and **Currency**. With no baseline and nothing recorded yet, the card asks "How much do you have right now?". The Add/Remove sheets show what the balance will be (in red below zero). Set Balance takes 0 or more in the UI (the rules and the data layer also take a negative baseline).
- **Goals** list, headed "Saving For" (top first, the order money fills them): name, cost, a progress bar (iridescent sheen once funded), "$1,550 to go · ≈ 6 weeks at your pace" or "Funded". "+" adds a goal (name, then Return goes on to the cost); tapping a goal edits it (name, cost, its progress, move up/down, delete, **Mark as Bought** when funded; Bought and Delete ask first). The moment a goal is first shown funded on a device it plays one prism sweep (none with Reduce Motion).

### 6.7 Task editing UI

## 8. Batch 2: agreed decisions (being built)

### 9.2 Custom times

**Rules of the game**
- The owner sets three moments of the week, in the space's clock: **Lists lock**, **Reviews open**, **Reviews due**. "Midnight" means the end of the day picked, so the usual times read "Lists lock Monday at midnight · Reviews open Thursday at midnight · Reviews due Friday at midnight".
- Constraints: lists lock before reviews open; the review window is at least 3 hours; reviews are due by Sunday at midnight. The earliest lock is "Sunday at midnight" (m = 0, the start of the week's Monday); lists can't lock earlier, e.g. on Sunday evening (§9.16). The app offers lock Sunday (midnight only) to Saturday and reviews Monday–Sunday, in 30-minute steps.
- A change needs a day's notice. It applies from the first week whose lists lock at least 24 hours from now on both its current times and the new ones: this week if that holds for it, else next week by the same test, else the week after. Earlier weeks keep their times forever (`RETIME_NOTICE_MS = 24 h`). Everyone else sees a notice until that week's deadline.
- New spaces start on the usual times; the creator changes them in Settings. Only the owner changes them; others see them read-only.

**Data**
```
spaces/{spaceId}           + schedule?:     { lock, reviewOpen, deadline }      # ints, minutes after Monday 00:00 local (wall clock)
                           + scheduleNote?: { by, at: ts, fromWeek, before: { lock, reviewOpen, deadline } }
spaces/{spaceId}/weeks/{w} + schedule?:     { lock, reviewOpen, deadline }      # the schedule its bounds follow
```
- Absent `schedule` = the usual `{ lock: 1440, reviewOpen: 5760, deadline: 7200 }` (`DEFAULT_SCHEDULE`). Clients never store the usual one ("Use the Usual Times" deletes the field on the space and on retimed weeks); the rules compare effective schedules, so an explicit default map is accepted too.
- Limits: ints, `0 ≤ lock < reviewOpen`, `reviewOpen + 180 ≤ deadline ≤ 10080`. That keeps a week's deadline at or before the next week's start, so weeks never overlap.
- Week docs without `schedule` (every week so far) follow the usual one. `scheduleNote.fromWeek` is range-checked by the rules (a Monday near the change), not proved; the job and screens use it as given.

**Rules**
- Helpers at top level: `defaultSchedule()`, `scheduleOf(d) = d.get('schedule', defaultSchedule())`, `validSchedule(s)` (the limits above, `exactKeys`), `followsSchedule(d, startAt, anchor, off, s)`: each bound within 1h of `startAt + minutes` and, with `utcOffsetMin`, within 1h of `anchor + minutes` (the two hours of slack can't stack). `monthStr` moves to top level. `notice() = duration.value(1, 'd')`.
- Space update `setsSchedule()`: `changedOnly(['schedule', 'scheduleNote'])`; writer is the owner and a member; `schedule` absent or valid; the effective schedule changes; `scheduleNote` exactly `{by, at, fromWeek, before}` with `by == auth.uid`, `at == request.time`, `before == scheduleOf(existing)`, and `fromWeek` a `YYYY-MM-DD` string that is a Monday (`timestamp.date(…).dayOfWeek() == 1`) between `request.time − 8d` and `request.time + 15d` (so a crafted client can't hide the notice behind an old week; about 15 expressions). No `get()`. No time window: retiming itself guards the weeks.
- Week create (`validNewWeek`, merged with judges, §9.4): required keys `startAt, lockAt, reviewOpenAt, deadlineAt, members, submittedBy`, optional `schedule` and `judges`, nothing else; `'schedule' in d ? d.schedule == scheduleOf(space) : scheduleOf(space) == defaultSchedule()`; `startAt` anchored as today; `followsSchedule(d, d.startAt, anchor, off, scheduleOf(space))`; with a `scheduleNote`, `d.lockAt ≥ note.at + notice()` (a week missing at the change can't be created on the new times with less than a day's notice; honest clients create this and next week before changing, §9.2 Data layer); the rest as today. A 6-key doc from an old client is accepted exactly as today in a space on the usual times (1440/5760/7200 minutes are 1/4/5 days).
- Week update `retimesWeek(spaceId, weekId)`, last in the update chain so existing branches cost what they cost: `changedOnly(['lockAt', 'reviewOpenAt', 'deadlineAt', 'schedule'])`; writer is a member (`getAfter(space)`, so the owner's change and the retimes go in one transaction); `request.time + notice() ≤ existing.lockAt` and `≤ incoming.lockAt` (a day's notice on both; every later bound follows its lock); `scheduleOf(existing) != s && scheduleOf(incoming) == s` for `s = scheduleOf(getAfter(space))`, so nobody can nudge a week within the hour of slack; `followsSchedule(incoming, existing.startAt, anchor, off, s)`. `startAt`, `members`, `submittedBy` never change here. About 75 expressions, 1 access.
- `validNewSpace` is unchanged for times (no `schedule` at create).

**Domain** (`time.ts`, new `schedule.ts`; pure)
- `time.ts`: `DEFAULT_SCHEDULE`, `WEEK_MINUTES = 10080`; `momentAt(weekId, tz, minutes)`: the instant `minutes` after the week's Monday 00:00 on the wall clock (whole days through `startOf('day')` exactly as `weekBounds` always did, so zones that skip midnight are unchanged; other times through `set({hour, minute})`: a time in a spring-forward gap moves forward, an ambiguous time takes its first occurrence; minutes < 0 or ≥ 10080 roll into the neighbouring weeks); `weekBounds(weekId, tz, schedule = DEFAULT_SCHEDULE)` (unchanged output for the usual schedule); `monthCompleteAt(monthId, tz, schedule = DEFAULT_SCHEDULE, lastWeek?)`: the stored `deadlineAt` of the month's last week when given, else computed on `schedule`.
- `schedule.ts`: `scheduleOf(x)` (absent → default), `sameSchedule(a?, b?)` (by effective values), `isUsualSchedule(s?)`, `scheduleProblem(s): 'shape' | 'order' | 'window' | 'range' | null` (null when the rules accept it), `scheduleProblemText(p)`, `splitMoment(m) → { day, time }` (day Monday = 0 … Sunday = 6, time in (0, 1440], 1440 = midnight = the end of that day; m = 0 → { day: −1, time: 1440 }), `joinMoment(day, time)`, `timeLabel(minuteOfDay, hourCycle?)` ("Midnight", "Noon", "6:00 PM" or "18:00"), `momentText(m, hourCycle?)` ("Monday at midnight", "Friday at 6:00 PM", "Saturday at noon"; m = 0 → "Sunday at midnight"), `scheduleChangeLines(before, after, hourCycle?)` (one line per moment that differs: "Lists now lock Monday at 8:00 PM", "Reviews now open …", "Reviews are now due Saturday at noon"), `boundsFor(space, weekId, week?)` (the stored bounds when the doc is given, else computed on the space's schedule), `weekScheduleOf(space, week?)` (the doc's schedule, absent = usual, else the space's), `RETIME_NOTICE_MS = 24 h`, `scheduleStartsWith(space, next, stored: { thisWeek?: Week | null; nextWeek?: Week | null }, nowMs) → WeekId` (as in Rules of the game: this week, next week or the week after, each tested with its stored lock, else its lock computed on the space's current schedule, against its lock on `next`; the week after always qualifies), `firstUnlockedWeek(space, thisWeek, nowMs) → WeekId` (this week while its stored or computed lock is ahead, else next week; for judges, no notice needed), `needsRetime(space, week, nowMs) → WeekBounds | null` (the same test as `retimesWeek`: another schedule, and the stored and new locks both at least `RETIME_NOTICE_MS` away). Every example schedule in this spec passes `scheduleProblem` (a test). The job uses `momentText`, so it lives in the domain.
- `scheduleProblemText(p, s?)`: order "Lists have to lock before reviews open."; window "Give reviews at least 3 hours."; range "Reviews have to be due by Sunday at midnight."; shape "Those times don’t work. Pick them again." For order and window, when one of `s`'s moments is a midnight, it adds " Midnight is the end of the day you pick."

**Data layer**
- `setSchedule(spaceId, schedule | null) → { fromWeek }` (new `src/data/schedule.ts`): validates with `scheduleProblem` and throws its text; fails at once offline (`requireServer`, exported from spaces.ts); reads the space from the server; non-owner → "Only the owner can change the times."; unchanged effective schedule → resolves without writing. - `boundsMismatch` compares a week with `weekBounds(weekId, tz, scheduleOf(week))`, its own schedule. The data layer's own window checks use the stored bounds (unchanged) and its messages stop naming days: "Adding tasks closed when reviews opened." and "Reviews haven’t opened yet."

### 9.3 Pause a week

**Rules of the game**
- Any member asks to pause **this week** (until its reviews open) or **next week**. It happens only if **everyone in that week who is still in the space** (judges too) says OK before that week's reviews open; before the week locks that is every current member, so someone who joins meanwhile must OK too; someone who joins after the lock isn't in the week and isn't asked; someone who leaves no longer counts. One **Not Now** ends the request. The asker can cancel it (it stays, marked as cancelled). One pause per month (the month of the week's Friday, as for scores). A completed pause is final. Each person can ask at most once every 12 hours, cancelled requests included. When nobody else needs to say OK, asking pauses at once.
- The app offers to ask only while that week's reviews open more than 24 hours from now (the rules allow it until they open): friends need time to answer, and the `pause-ask` push can be hours late.
- In a paused week nothing counts: no score, no reviews needed, no missed-review points (weekly or monthly, §9.5), and it is left out of the month's average (not 0%) and out of streaks. Lists, ticks, adds and money still work, just for you. Its stored times don't change.

**Data** (on the space doc, so no new reads anywhere)
```
spaces/{spaceId}  + pause?:  { weekId, by, at: ts, oks: [uid…], declinedBy?: uid }   # the one request in play
                  + paused?: { [monthId]: weekId }                                     # completed pauses
```
- States are derived: *recorded* when `paused[monthOfWeek(weekId)] == weekId`; *withdrawn* when `declinedBy == by` (the asker cancelled); *declined* when `declinedBy` is someone else; *lapsed* when not recorded and now ≥ that week's (stored) `reviewOpenAt`; else *pending*. A withdrawn request is kept (so the asker's 12-hour wait still holds). A paused week is a week in `space.paused`, nothing else; week docs are untouched.
- Who must say OK: `needed = week.members ∩ space.memberIds` (the week doc always exists: asking creates it first). A request completes when `oks ⊇ needed`.

**Rules** (space update branches; inside `match /spaces/{spaceId}`; helpers `monthOfWeek(weekId)` = `monthStr` of its Friday, `beforeReviews(weekId)` = `request.time < get(week).reviewOpenAt` (the week doc must exist), `neededOk(oks, w, memberIds)` = `oks.toSet().hasAll(w.members.toSet().intersection(memberIds.toSet()))` on that same cached week doc (about 5 expressions, no extra access), `recordsPauseIf(done, weekId)`, `requestOver(p)`):
- `asksToPause()`: a member; `pause` exactly `{weekId, by, at, oks}` with `by == auth.uid`, `at == request.time`, `oks == [auth.uid]`, a `YYYY-MM-DD` week id; the previous request is over (none, declined or withdrawn, recorded, or past its week's review opening); not within 12h of the same person's previous request; `beforeReviews`; the month has no pause yet; when `neededOk([auth.uid], …)` the same write records `paused[month] = weekId`, else only `pause` changes.
- `oksPause()`: a member not yet in `oks`, request not declined; `oks` becomes `oks + [auth.uid]`, the other fields unchanged; `beforeReviews`; when `neededOk` holds after it, the same write must record `paused[month]` (a completing OK without it is refused, so a pause is never complete but unrecorded, and two racing final OKs record it once).
- `finalizesPause()`: a member; request not declined; `pause` unchanged; `neededOk`; `beforeReviews`; records `paused[month]` (the last holdout left).
- `declinesPause()`: `changedOnly(['pause'])`; not declined; month not yet paused; the request gains exactly `declinedBy: auth.uid`; the writer is a member not in `oks` (Not Now), or the asker `pause.by`, still a member (cancelling: withdrawn). There is no delete branch.
- `recordsPauseIf`: `!(month in paused before)` and, when recording, `changedOnly(['pause', 'paused'])` with only `paused[month]` added and equal to the week; else `changedOnly(['pause'])`.
- Every other space branch already excludes `pause`/`paused` through `changedOnly`. Week, plan, progress and review rules are untouched. About 50–80 expressions per branch, at most 2 accesses.

**Domain** (new `pause.ts`)
- `PAUSE_COOLDOWN_MS = 12 h`; `PAUSE_ASK_LEAD_MS = 24 h`; `isWeekPaused(space, weekId)` (`paused[monthIdForWeek(weekId)] === weekId`); `pausedWeekOf(space, monthId)`; `pauseNeeded(memberIds, weekMembers)` (current members in the week); `pauseCompletes(oks, memberIds, weekMembers)`; `pauseView(space, me, nowMs, week: { members, reviewOpenAt, deadlineAt }) → { kind: 'none' } | { kind: 'pending', request, waitingFor, me: 'asked' | 'said-ok' | 'to-answer' | 'not-asked' } | { kind: 'declined', request, by } | { kind: 'paused', weekId } (until that week's deadline) | { kind: 'lapsed', request, waitingFor }` (a withdrawn request is `none`; `waitingFor` = needed members not in `oks`); `pauseChoices(space, me, nowMs, { thisWeek, nextWeek }) → { thisWeek, nextWeek }` each `'ok' | 'too-late' | 'reviews-open' | 'month-used' | 'request-pending' | 'cooldown'` ('too-late': reviews open in ≤ `PAUSE_ASK_LEAD_MS`); `pauseNeedsFinalizing(space, nowMs, week)`. The `week` arguments give stored bounds and members when the doc is known, else `boundsFor` and the current members.
- Scoring: §9.6.

**Data layer** (new `src/data/pause.ts`): `askToPause(space, weekId) → 'asked' | 'paused'` (ensures the week doc first, pre-checks with `pauseChoices`), `answerPause(space, 'ok' | 'not-now') → 'waiting' | 'paused' | 'declined'`, `withdrawPause(space)` (writes `declinedBy` = me, the asker), `finalizePause(space)` (in `ensureSpace`; returns at once unless needed). No reviews, no score, and it’s left out of October’s average."; your list as usual with the footer "Paused: your list is just for you this week." ("next week" on the Next Week tab), replacing the planning footer's 0% and lock warnings; cards without score rings, "So far" or review lines; no reveal glow; no Review toolbar **unless** `reviewParts(…).monthly.open` (the month's last week, §9.5): then the toolbar reads as usual for the monthly part and the status card gains a row **Review the October Lists** · "No points this week, just the monthly lists." that opens the review sheet on its monthly sections; otherwise the review sheet shows "This week is paused" · "There’s nothing to review.". ### 9.4 Judges

**Rules of the game**
- Each member decides for themselves whether they **only rate** (**Only Rate Friends**, in Settings, and when creating or joining a space). It applies from the next week (and monthly list) that hasn't locked; a locked period keeps the role it locked with.
- A judge has no list, no score and nothing at stake; reviews everyone with tasks; is never reviewed; the reveal waits for them like for anyone (until the deadline). A judge who skips reviews shows as **Didn't review** and loses nothing. Judges count toward the 8-member cap and toward a pause's OKs.
- **Switching mid-month.** In a month where someone also plays (at least one week they didn't judge, or a monthly list with tasks), every week they judged (not paused) counts as **0%** in their average, like a week with nothing planned. A month someone only judged has no score and is skipped for streaks. So becoming a judge can't hide bad weeks, and judging most of a month can't turn one easy week into a pass.

**Data**
```
spaces/{spaceId}                 + judges?: [uid…]   # each member's own choice; absent = nobody
spaces/{spaceId}/weeks/{w}       + judges?: [uid…]   # snapshot of the space's, kept equal until lockAt
spaces/{spaceId}/monthly/{m}       judges:  [uid…]   # always present (§9.5)
```
- Who judges a period: `judgesOf(period) = judges ∩ members`; a week doc without `judges` (from before batch 2) has none. The space's list may hold stale entries of people who left (an old client's leave doesn't drop them): every reader intersects with members (`spaceJudges(space)`), and joining sets or clears the joiner's own entry (Data layer below).

**Rules**
- `ownJudgeOnly(uids)`: `incoming.get('judges', [])` minus `uids` equals `existing.get('judges', [])` minus `uids`, and grows by at most `uids.size()` (each added once).
- `validNewSpace`: the 12 keys as today, or those plus `judges` with `judges == []` or `[auth.uid]`.
- `joinsSpace`: `changedOnly(['memberIds', 'joinedAt', 'judges']) && ownJudgeOnly([uid])`; `leavesSpace`: adds `'judges'` to `changedOnly` with `ownJudgeOnly([uid])`; `removesMember`: adds it with `ownJudgeOnly(removed)`. Old clients don't touch `judges`, so the check holds for them.
- New space branch `setsOwnJudge()`: a member, `changedOnly(['judges'])`, `ownJudgeOnly([auth.uid])`.
- Week create: `'judges' in d ? d.judges == space.get('judges', []) : space.get('judges', []).size() == 0`. A doc without a snapshot (an old client) is accepted only in a space without judges, so nobody can make a judge play with an empty list (0%).
- New period update branch `syncsPeriod(spaceId)` (weeks and monthly lists): `changedOnly(['members', 'judges'])`, before `existing.lockAt`, writer a member of `getAfter(space)`, members only appended (current members, no duplicates), `judges == space.get('judges', [])`. `addsMembers` stays for old clients.
- Plans: `canPlan` and `appendsTask` also require `!(uid in w.get('judges', []))` (about 8 expressions on the ~890 plan path). It is defence in depth: the domain ignores a judge's plan anyway. If the measured headroom of the full-plan test drops below 40 expressions, drop it from `canPlan` (keep it in `appendsTask`).

**Domain**: `judgesOf(period)`, `spaceJudges(space)` in `scoring.ts`; results in §9.6.

**Data layer**: new clients write the old shapes when nobody judges, so creating, joining and planning keep working even if the rules were published late: `createSpace({ …, judge? })` writes `judges: [me]` only when judging (else no `judges` key); `joinSpace(code, { judge? })` adds `judges: arrayUnion(me)` to the join batch only when judging; when not judging and the space still lists me (a stale entry from an earlier stay, left by an old client), one `setsOwnJudge` write removes it right after the join; week docs carry `judges` only when the space's list isn't empty. Leaving, removal and `deleteMyAccount` add `arrayRemove(uid)` when the space lists them, and all three (through the shared `leaveUnlockedWeeks`) also take the user out of this month's and next week's month's monthly lists that haven't locked (`leavesWeek` works on them), in the same batch as the space update. `setJudge(spaceId, judge)`: a transaction that writes the space's `judges` (drop me, then append me if judging) and the same list, plus missing members, to every existing week and monthly list that hasn't locked (this week, next week, this month's and next week's month's lists); if refused (a lock just passed) it retries with the space alone; offline `MSG.offline`. `ensureWeek`/`ensureMonthly` keep unlocked periods' `judges` equal to the space's (one `update` with `judges` and `arrayUnion` of missing members). Plans: `savePlan`/`addTaskAfterLock` for a weekly judge throw "You’re judging this week, so there’s no list to plan."; `saveMonthlyPlan` for a monthly judge "You’re judging this month’s lists, so there’s no list to plan."

**Screens and copy**

### 9.5 Monthly lists

**Rules of the game**
- Each month can have an optional **monthly list** per player: bigger things that take the month. It is planned from 8 days before the month's first week until it **locks with the month's second week's list**. After that it can't change or grow (no adds), and it is ticked until its deadline. Steps work as in §6.1; no revenue switch.
- It is reviewed in the **month's last week's review window**, by everyone in the list (judges too), blind like weeks, in its own submit. It is scored exactly like a week; a player with no tasks at the lock simply has no monthly list (no extra week, not 0%) but still reviews the others.
- A closed monthly list with ≥ 1 task counts as **one extra week** in that month's average. Missed monthly reviews cost 5 points each, except for monthly judges and when the month's last week is paused (§9.3: then the monthly list is still reviewed by whoever does; with no review it falls back to self check-offs, and it still counts).
- Who is in it follows the lock rule: everyone in the space before its lock, minus those who left before it; leaving after it keeps you in. Judges: the space's judges, snapshot until its lock.

**Data**
```
spaces/{id}/monthly/{monthId}                    { startAt, lockAt, reviewOpenAt, deadlineAt, members[], judges[], submittedBy[] }
spaces/{id}/monthly/{monthId}/plans/{uid}        { tasks: [{ id, title, subs? }], updatedAt }           # never `added`
spaces/{id}/monthly/{monthId}/progress/{uid}     { done: { [id]: boolean }, updatedAt }
spaces/{id}/monthly/{monthId}/reviews/{rid}      { reviewerId, revieweeId, marks, submittedAt }
```
- `monthId` `YYYY-MM`. With `W = weekIdsInMonth(monthId)` (4 or 5 weeks): `startAt` = `W[0]`'s start, `lockAt` = `W[1]`'s lock, `reviewOpenAt`/`deadlineAt` = `W[last]`'s review window: the stored bounds of those weeks when their docs exist, else computed on the space's schedule. Usual times, October 2026: Mon Sep 28 00:00; Tue Oct 6 00:00; Fri Oct 30 00:00 → Sat Oct 31 00:00. Caps as a week's.
- The collection is `monthly` (not `months`, which holds the frozen stakes).

**Rules**
- Shared period functions with a **literal** collection name passed from two thin match blocks (`weeks/{weekId}` and `monthly/{monthId}`), never a `{period}` wildcard (it would cost the plan path a guard and could expose other collections): `periodPath(spaceId, coll, id)`; `canPlan(spaceId, coll, id, uid)`, `canTick`, `validReview` take it; `appendsTask` stays weeks-only. `addsMembers`, `leavesWeek`, `submitsReviews`, `revealed`, `validTasks`, `validProgress`, `validMarkKeys` are reused unchanged.
- `monthly/{monthId}`: read by members; create `validNewMonthly`; update `syncsPeriod || submitsReviews || leavesWeek || retimesMonthly`; `plans/{uid}`: create/update by `canPlan(…'monthly'…)` with `{tasks, updatedAt}` only (no append branch), delete before the lock; `progress` and `reviews` as weeks with `'monthly'`.
- `monthAnchors(space, monthId)`: from `monthId` alone (`timestamp.date`, `dayOfWeek()`): the Monday of the week holding the month's first Friday, the Monday of its last week (7 days before the next month's first), their `YYYY-MM-DD` ids (`lockId` = first + 7 days, `lastId`), minus `utcOffsetMin` (required: a space without it gets no monthly list), and `scheduleOf(space)`.
- `validNewMonthly`: `monthId` matches `^[0-9]{4}-(0[1-9]|1[0-2])$`; exactly the 7 keys; `startAt` within 1h of the first anchor; `lockAt` within 1h of first + 10080 + `lock` minutes; `reviewOpenAt`/`deadlineAt` within 1h of last + `reviewOpen`/`deadline` minutes; `submittedBy == []`; `request.time < lockAt`; `startAt < request.time + 8d`; `members` exactly the space's members; `judges == space.get('judges', [])`. About 170 expressions, 1 access (+1 for `isMember`).
- `retimesMonthly`: a member of `getAfter(space)`; `changedOnly(['lockAt', 'reviewOpenAt', 'deadlineAt'])`; if the lock moves: old and new lock both at least `notice()` (24h) ahead, and the new lock equals `getAfter(weeks/lockId).lockAt` when that week doc exists, else within 1h of its anchor on `scheduleOf(getAfter(space))`; if the review window moves: old and new opening both at least `notice()` ahead, and it equals `getAfter(weeks/lastId)`'s stored window when that doc exists, else (only while `request.time < last + lock − 1h`) within 1h of its anchors. ≤ 5 accesses. Tying to the stored weeks (after this batch) keeps the list exactly on its weeks once they exist.
- A full 25 × 8 monthly plan batched with `syncsPeriod` must fit like the weekly one (test).

**Domain**
- `time.ts`: `monthlyWeeks(monthId) → { first, lock, last }`; `monthlyBounds(monthId, boundsOf: (weekId) => WeekBounds) → WeekBounds`; `isMonthlyReviewWeek(weekId)`.
- `scoring.ts`: `computeMonthly(data: MonthlyData, space, nowMs): WeekResult` (`weekId` holds the monthId; like `computeWeek` with `counted = membersAt(space, monthly.lockAt)` and the list's judges; zero tasks → `score: null`, `provisionalScore: null`, `taskCount: 0`; never paused). `reviewTargets(data: WeekData | MonthlyData, uid, memberIds?)`: the period's members (or `memberIds`) other than `uid` with ≥ 1 task, never its judges (`judgesOf(period)`; a judge's leftover plan is ignored, so nobody is charged for not reviewing a judge).

**Data layer** (`src/data/monthly.ts`; it may share code with weeks.ts)
- `startMonthly`/`useMonthly(space, monthId | null)`: like `watchWeek` (blind reveal, timer at the deadline, never revealed with only your reviews), key `${uid}|monthly|${spaceId}|${monthId}`; `boundsMismatch` against `monthlyBounds` of the stored/computed weeks.
- `ensureMonthly(space, monthId)`, run by `ensureSpace` for this week's month and, when different, next week's: skip when the list has locked and needs no retime, or when its start is more than 8 days away; else a transaction: create it (`expected` bounds, all current members, the space's judges, `submittedBy: []`); or retime it (one write; the lock part and the review part each only when it differs by more than 60 s and `retimesMonthly` allows it, as in `setSchedule`); or sync members/judges while unlocked. When the owner runs it on an unlocked list, it also takes out members who are no longer in the space (one `leavesWeek` write each; old clients leave them behind), so the monthly reveal isn't held up until the deadline. One retry. Its cache key covers members, judges and schedule.
- `saveMonthlyPlan(space, monthId, tasks)` (as `savePlan`, no earnings; after the lock `MSG.closed`; a monthly judge: the judge message; not in the list: "This month’s list started without you. You’re in from next month."), `setMonthlyTaskDone(space, monthId, id, done)` (as `setTaskDone`, own queue key `${uid}|${spaceId}|m:${monthId}`, no earnings, the 200-key cleanup), `submitMonthlyReviews(space, monthId, reviews)` (as `submitReviews`; before the window "Monthly lists are reviewed in the month’s last week."; after it `MSG.closed`).
- `useMonthData(space, monthId)`: the month's weeks plus its list, loaded only from the computed start of the month's last week (before that the extra week can't count): key `${uid}|month|${spaceId}|${monthId}|m`. `useMonth` is deprecated and goes once no screen uses it.
- `forgetSpace` drops `${uid}|monthly|${spaceId}|…` too.

### 9.6 Scoring with all of it

- `computeWeek(data, space, nowMs)` (signature unchanged): bounds = the stored doc, else `boundsFor(space, weekId)`; `paused = isWeekPaused(space, weekId)`; judges = `judgesOf(week)`. A judge's result: `participating: true, judge: true, taskCount: 0, tasks: [], provisionalScore: null, score: null, reviewedBy: []`, `missedReviewsOf` = targets they didn't review after the deadline, `hasSubmitted`. A judge's plan is ignored and reviews **of** a judge are dropped; reviews **by** a judge count. In a paused week every `score` is null and every `missedReviewsOf` is []; `tasks`, `provisionalScore`, `hasSubmitted` and `revealed` as usual (screens check `paused` first).
- Result types gain fields that are **optional in the type** (absent = false / [] / null), so code that builds results by hand keeps compiling; the domain always sets them: `MemberWeekResult.judge?`, `WeekResult.paused?`, `WeekResult.judges?`, `MemberMonthResult.weeks[].paused?: true`, `MemberMonthResult.judged?: WeekId[]`, `.monthly?: { score: number | null; closed: boolean; taskCount: number } | null`, `.monthlyJudge?: boolean`, `.skippedAsJudge?: number`.
- `computeMonth(space, monthId, weeks, nowMs, monthly?: MonthlyData)`: for each member of the roster (current members ∪ week participants ∪ list participants):
  - each week with a doc where they participate: paused → listed `{ paused: true }`, not averaged, no misses; judged → in `judged`, its misses (after its deadline) add to `skippedAsJudge`; else listed, and when closed with a score, averaged and its misses counted;
  - the monthly list (`computeMonthly`): a monthly judge → `monthlyJudge`, closed misses to `skippedAsJudge`; a player with ≥ 1 task → `monthly` entry, and once closed (now ≥ its deadline) with a score, one more averaged score; a player's closed misses count (−5 each) whether or not they had a list, unless the month's last week is paused;
  - **judged weeks in a played month**: when the member is a player of the month (a non-paused week they didn't judge, or a `monthly` entry), each judged, non-paused week averages as **0** once closed (listed as judged with `score: 0`; its misses still go to `skippedAsJudge`, not to the penalty). A judge-only month keeps no score;
  - `average`, `penaltyPoints = latePenalty × missed`, `finalScore`, `passed` as today; `complete = nowMs ≥ monthEndsAt(space, monthId, weeks, monthly)`;
  - `others` = players other than them, players being those with a non-paused played week or a monthly entry (judge-only members and late joiners are nobody's `others`).
- `monthEndsAt(space, monthId, weeks, monthly?)`: the later of the last week's stored `deadlineAt` (computed with `boundsFor` without a doc) and the monthly list's `deadlineAt`.
- With no monthly list, no judges and no pause, every result equals today's (the existing scoring tests stay as they are, plus the new fields).
- Screens decide review targets with `membersAt(space, period.lockAt)`, as scoring and the job do. (Before batch 2 the screens used `deadlineAt`, so a friend who left after the lock was never offered for review yet missing them cost 5 points: fixed.) "Waiting for" lists name only current members (`stillIn`).
- **Waiting on someone who left**: when every current member of a period has submitted but it isn't revealed (a member who left after the lock still holds it until the deadline), screens say "Results come in at the deadline, {deadlineText(deadlineAt)}. A friend who left can’t review anymore." (review sheet success page, `ReviewStatus`, the monthly part), never "Everyone’s in" or "Results are live" unless the period is revealed.

### 9.7 Per-person stakes and settling

**Rules of the game**
- **House stakes.** The space's stakes are its **house stakes**; only the owner changes them (Settings). They count between two people who haven't agreed on their own. An owner's edit counts **at once** for the month in progress (`termsMonth`: this week's month, or the next one once that month is complete). A month's house stakes are fixed when it is **complete** (`monthEndsAt`: its last week's stored deadline, or its monthly list's if later): `months/{monthId}` is created then, copying the space's, by whichever comes first: a member opening the app (`ensureSpace`), the owner's next edit (which freezes a finished month in the same write, before changing the stakes; the rules insist on it, so even a crafted client can't change a finished month's house stakes) or the reminders job (when it writes the month's results). **Early freezes.** The previous app froze each month at its first lock. Months before `FIRST_RESULTS_MONTH` (October 2026, the month batch 2 ships in, §9.8) keep what it froze: they are complete and settled under it. From October 2026 on, a freeze made before the month was complete doesn't bind (`freezeBinds`, Domain below): until the month is complete its house stakes are the space's current ones, so an owner's edit in October counts at once like any other, and at completion the early freeze is overwritten once with the space's stakes (the rules allow exactly that update). So the month whose terms count now always has the space's house stakes, on every screen.
- **Your own stakes.** Any member can set their own: the **reward line** (what friends owe you when you hit the bar) and the **penalty line** (what you owe when you miss), each either **Same for everyone** (one thing the group owes you together, or one thing you give the group) or **Different per friend**; plus up to 5 streak rewards, one list for the whole group (§9.8).
- **Agreeing.** Each friend taps **Agree** on a member's current stakes, exactly as shown; the Agree is stored with its server time. There is no "decline": not agreeing keeps the old terms.
- **What counts, and when.** A month's terms between M and a friend come from the agreements made **before the month was complete**. So an agreed change counts at once for the month in progress, one agreed after a month is complete counts from the next month, and a finished month never changes (later agreements have later times). Each line is decided on its own (`memberTerms`, Domain below):
  - a **Same-for-everyone** line and the **streak rewards** count once **every friend** who plays that month and was in the space when it was agreed has agreed to it; until then what they all last agreed to keeps counting, else the house stakes;
  - a **Different-per-friend** line counts with that friend's own Agree, once everyone who has agreed has that line per friend (a switch between the two modes waits for all of them, so nobody ends up owing a "together" text alone);
  - a friend who joined after the agreement that counts, or who never agreed, is on the house stakes for that line (together, no streak rewards).
- The owner accepts that friends can agree late in a month to soften someone's stakes (§9.15). Everyone sees stakes change: a friend's new stakes on everyone's stakes card, a house-stakes change as a notice, and pushes (§9.11).
- When a month is complete, for each player M and each friend F in M's `others`: M passed → F owes M the reward line in force; M missed → M owes F the penalty line in force. Both directions appear. Lines group friends on the same text and source (a Same-for-everyone line, or the house stakes).
- Once the month's results doc exists (§9.8), its stored terms are the month's terms for good (screens and the job read them, not the stakes docs).
- Settling is per pair and honor-system. A line owed by or to several people together is one tap that marks every pair on it.

**Data**
```
spaces/{id}                + stakesNote?: { by, at: ts }    # the last house-stakes change (an owner on a batch-2 app)
spaces/{id}/stakes/{uid}   { rewardMode: 'group'|'each', penaltyMode: 'group'|'each', reward, penalty,
                             per: { [friend]: { reward, penalty } }, tiers: [{ months, reward }],
                             agreed: { [friend]: { at: ts, terms: PairTerms, past: [AgreedTerms…] } }, updatedAt }
                           PairTerms   = { rewardMode, penaltyMode, reward, penalty, tiers }   # an exact copy of the pair's terms
                           AgreedTerms = PairTerms + { at: ts }
spaces/{id}/months/{monthId}   { reward, penalty, createdAt }   # unchanged shape; now created once the month is complete
                                                                # (an early freeze from 2026-10 on is overwritten then, once)
spaces/{id}/settlements/{monthId}_{uid}_{other}             { monthId, uid, other, settled, settledBy, settledAt }
spaces/{id}/settlements/{monthId}_{uid}_{other}_s{months}   { monthId, uid, other, tier, settled, settledBy, settledAt }
```
- The stakes doc id is its owner's uid; created on first Save; never deleted (months already played need it, former members' too). Pair terms for friend F: `{ rewardMode, penaltyMode, reward: rewardMode == 'group' ? reward : per[F].reward, penalty: likewise, tiers }`; none (`'missing'`) when a line is per friend and `per[F]` is absent.
- `agreed[F]`: the current agreement is `terms`, made at `at` (server time; arrays can't hold server timestamps, so the latest time lives outside the list). Each new Agree moves the current one to the end of `past` as `{ ...terms, at }`, dropping the oldest beyond 5 (6 agreements in all), and is only allowed when the terms differ from the current ones. `past` is oldest first, `at` strictly ascending.
- Limits: texts ≤ 120; `reward` (or `penalty`) is 1–120 and not blank while its line is Same for everyone, and may be '' while it is per friend (kept for switching back); a `per` entry has both texts (0–120), and the text of a per-friend line is 1–120 and not blank; ≤ 7 `per` entries keyed by current members other than the owner; ≤ 5 tiers, months ints 2–36 strictly ascending, reward 1–120 not blank.
- Settlement shapes: the legacy `{monthId}_{uid}` doc (§2) still settles every non-tier debt whose subject is `uid` in that month, unless a pair doc says otherwise; a pair doc decides its pair; a legacy doc never covers a streak reward. Uids have no `_`, so ids can't collide.
- No stakes docs before batch 2: every pair reads as the house stakes, exactly as today.

**Rules**
- `match /stakes/{uid}`: read by members; create by `uid` themself, a member, `validStakes`, `agreed == {}`; update `editsOwnStakes || agreesToStakes`; no delete.
- `validStakes(uid, d, memberIds)`: exact keys `rewardMode, penaltyMode, reward, penalty, per, tiers, agreed, updatedAt`; each mode in group/each; `reward`/`penalty` 0–120, and 1–120 not blank when its mode is group; `per` a map, ≤ 7 keys, `hasOnly(memberIds)`, not `uid`, each exactly `{reward, penalty}` with texts 0–120, `reward` 1–120 not blank when `rewardMode == 'each'` and `penalty` likewise (7 explicit slots); `tiers` a list of ≤ 5 `{months, reward}`, months int 2–36 strictly ascending (5 slots); `agreed` a map; `updatedAt == request.time`.
- `editsOwnStakes`: `isUser(uid)`, a member, `changedOnly(['rewardMode', 'penaltyMode', 'reward', 'penalty', 'per', 'tiers', 'updatedAt'])` (never `agreed`), `validStakes`. About 310 expressions, 1 access.
- `agreesToStakes(spaceId, owner)`: the writer isn't the owner and is a member; `changedOnly(['agreed'])` and only `agreed[me]` changes; `agreed[me]` exactly `{at, terms, past}` with `at == request.time`; `terms` exactly `{rewardMode, penaltyMode, reward, penalty, tiers}` and equal to the doc's current pair terms for me (with `me in per` when either line is per friend), so terms that changed a moment earlier refuse the write; with no earlier agreement `past == []`; else `terms != old.terms` (no unchanged re-agree) and `past` is `old.past` plus the old agreement stamped with its time (`s.diff(old.terms).affectedKeys() == ['at']`, `s.at == old.at`), without `old.past[0]` when `old.past` already holds 5. No month or freeze check: the time is the server's, and which month it counts for is the domain's. About 60 expressions, 1 access.
- `monthCompleteBy(spaceId, space, monthId, t, slack)` (top level, shared by the two rules below so both use one instant): with `lastId` the month's last week (`monthAnchors`, from the month alone), `t ≥ weeks/{lastId}.deadlineAt` when that doc exists (`exists` + `get`), else `t ≥` its anchor + `scheduleOf(space).deadline` minutes + `slack` (a space without `utcOffsetMin`: `monthStr(t − 4d) > monthId`, complete in any zone on any schedule); and `!exists(monthly/{monthId}) || t ≥ get(monthly/{monthId}).deadlineAt`. ≤ 2 accesses. `FIRST_RESULTS_MONTH` is the literal `'2026-10'` here (kept equal to the domain's, §9.8).
- `editsSpaceText`: the owner may change `name`, `reward`, `penalty` and `stakesNote`; other members only `name` (old clients send all three; unchanged values aren't changes, so a non-owner's rename still passes and a non-owner's stakes edit is refused). `stakesNote` may change only together with `reward` or `penalty`, and then is exactly `{by, at}` with `by == auth.uid` and `at == request.time`; an owner's edit without it (an old client) still passes. **A finished month is frozen first**: only when `reward` or `penalty` changes, for `m` in C = `monthStr(request.time)` and P = the month before C (`monthStr` of C's first day − 1 day; a month completes between its 18th and the next month's 3rd, so these two hold every month that can have completed recently), each `m ≥ monthStr(existing().createdAt)` must satisfy `!monthCompleteBy(m, request.time, +1h) || (existsAfter(months/m) && (m < '2026-10' || monthCompleteBy(m, getAfter(months/m).createdAt, −1h)))`: a complete month has a freeze in this batch or before, and from 2026-10 on one made at completion (not the previous app's early one). Renames pay nothing; a stakes change about 150 more expressions and ≤ 6 accesses (each month's last week, list and `months` doc; the space is `resource`).
- `validMonthStakes` (`months/{monthId}` create; replaces today's month window): exactly `{reward, penalty, createdAt}`; `reward`/`penalty` equal to `get(space)`'s (the state before the request, so the owner's edit batch freezes the old stakes); `createdAt == request.time`; `monthId` matches `YYYY-MM` and is ≥ the month of `request.time − 62d` (recent months only); and the month is **complete**: `monthCompleteBy(monthId, request.time, −1h)`. About 80 expressions, ≤ 3 accesses. An old client's freeze at the first lock is now refused (its upkeep ignores the refusal); the month is frozen when it ends. The hour of slack each way (allowed from −1h, insisted on from +1h, only when the last week has no doc) covers the anchor's DST error; with the stored doc both are its exact deadline.
- `months/{monthId}` update (**re-freezing an early freeze**, the only update): a member, `monthId >= '2026-10'`, `validMonthStakes`, and `!monthCompleteBy(monthId, resource.data.createdAt, −1h)` (the stored freeze was made before the month was complete: the previous app's freeze at its first lock). Since the new `createdAt` is after completion, it happens once. No delete. Old clients never update these docs.
- `validSettlement`: one of the three exact key sets; `monthId` `YYYY-MM`; `other` (when present) a string ≠ `uid`, a current member or in `leftAt`; `tier` (when present, only with `other`) an int 2–36; `sid == monthId + '_' + uid (+ '_' + other) (+ '_s' + string(tier))`; writer a member; `uid` a current member or in `leftAt`; `settled` bool; `settledBy == auth.uid`; `settledAt == request.time`. About 45 expressions and 1 access per doc (a group line of 7 in one batch: 7 ≤ 20).
- `invites` (`mirrorsSpace`) is unchanged.

**Domain** (new `stakes.ts`; pure, Node-runnable)
- Constants: `MAX_TIERS = 5`, `MIN_TIER_MONTHS = 2`, `MAX_TIER_MONTHS = 36`, `TIER_CHOICES = [2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24]`, `MAX_PAST = 5`.
- `pairTerms(stakes, friend): PairTerms | null`; `sameTerms(a, b)` (modes, texts, tiers in order); `agreementsOf(stakes, friend): AgreedTerms[]` (`past`, then the current one with its `at`; [] without an Agree); `agreementStatus(stakes, friend): 'agreed' | 'waiting' | 'missing'` (by content: the current agreement equals the current pair terms, so going back to terms a friend agreed to reads as agreed; 'missing' without pair terms); `waitingFor(stakes, friends)` (the `'waiting'` ones), `missingFor(stakes, friends)`.
- `nextAgreement(prev: Agreement | undefined, terms: PairTerms): { terms, past } | null`: exactly what the rules accept (null when `terms` equal `prev.terms`: already agreed, nothing to write); the data layer adds `at: serverTimestamp()`.
- `wasMemberAt(space, uid, ms)`: a current member who joined at or before `ms`, or a former member who left after `ms` (their join time is gone, so assumed earlier). Someone who left and rejoined counts from their latest join.
- `memberTerms(space, subject, friends, stakes, house, cutoffMs) → Record<UID, TermsInForce>`: what counts between `subject` and each of `friends`, from the agreements made before `cutoffMs` (`at < cutoffMs`); `house = { reward, penalty }`. Each line (reward, penalty, tiers) on its own:
  1. Candidate times: the `at` of every counted agreement of `friends` to `subject`, latest first. At a candidate `t`: `G_t` = the `friends` who were in the space at `t` (`wasMemberAt`); `E_t` = those of `G_t` with an agreement at or before `t` (their latest such one is "in force at `t`"); `N_t` = the rest of `G_t`.
  2. The reward line is **settled at `t`** when `E_t` isn't empty, every agreement in force at `t` has the same `rewardMode` and, when that mode is `group`, `N_t` is empty and they all have the same `reward`. Likewise the penalty line. The tiers are settled at `t` when `E_t` isn't empty, `N_t` is empty and every agreement in force has the same `tiers`.
  3. The latest `t` at which a line is settled decides it: each friend in `E_t` gets that line from their agreement in force at `t` (`source: 'agreed'`, `at` its time; `together` for a group line and for tiers); every other friend gets the house line (`together: true`, `source: 'house'`) and no tiers. A line settled nowhere is the house line for everyone (tiers []).
  So a Same-for-everyone change counts once everyone needed has agreed, a per-friend change counts with that friend's Agree, and a switch between the modes waits for everyone who had agreed before.
- `termsMonth(space, nowMs, thisWeek?: Week | null, monthly?: MonthlyList | null): MonthId`: the month whose terms count now: this week's month (`monthIdForWeek`), or the next one once that month is complete (`monthEndsAt`, with this week's stored doc as its last week and the month's list).
- `termsFriends(space, me, firstLockAt, judges = spaceJudges(space)) → UID[]`: whose agreement a month in progress waits for, as near to its `others` as the space doc tells: current members other than `me` not in `judges`, plus former members (`leftAt`, no longer members) who left at or after the month's first lock (`firstLockAt`: its first week's stored lock, else computed), since a friend who played part of the month and left still owes or is owed for it. Both the stakes card and the month view of a month that isn't complete use it, so they agree (but for the rare cases in §9.15); a complete month uses its `others`.
- `monthTerms(space, monthId, month: Record<UID, MemberMonthResult>, stakes, house, at: { completeAt, firstLockAt }, stored?: MonthResults | null) → Record<UID, Record<UID, TermsInForce>>` (subject → friend): `stored.terms` when present; else, once the month is complete (`month[…].complete`), `memberTerms(space, subject, others, stakes, house, completeAt)` for each player; before that, `memberTerms(space, subject, termsFriends(space, subject, firstLockAt) ∪ others, …)` (every agreement so far counts, which is what counts now). `house` = `monthHouse(…)` below; `completeAt` = `monthEndsAt`; `firstLockAt` = the month's first week's stored lock, else computed.
- `tiersInForce(terms, subject)`: the tiers of any of the subject's pairs whose tiers aren't empty (they all come from the one time the tiers were settled, so they are equal), else []. Pairs can carry [] beside them (a friend who wasn't among those who settled the tiers, e.g. a newcomer who agreed only to new tiers the others haven't), so "any agreed pair" would wrongly drop a run.
- **House stakes per month**: `HOUSE_SLACK_MS = 2 h` (the rules' hour of slack plus the anchor's DST hour). `freezeBinds(monthId, frozen: MonthStakes, completeAt)`: `monthId < FIRST_RESULTS_MONTH`, or `frozen.createdAt` missing or `≥ completeAt − HOUSE_SLACK_MS` (a freeze made at completion; the previous app's freeze at the first lock doesn't bind from `FIRST_RESULTS_MONTH` on). `monthHouse(space, monthId, frozen: MonthStakes | null | undefined, completeAt) → { reward, penalty, frozen: boolean }`: the freeze when it binds, else the space's current text. `houseFreezeAt(space, monthId, lastWeek?, monthly?)`: from when the rules accept the month's freeze and, on an owner's stakes edit, insist on it: `monthEndsAt` (stored last week and list; computed without the doc), except for a space without `utcOffsetMin` and no stored last week: the 5th of the next month, 00:00 UTC (the rules' fallback). `needsHouseFreeze(monthId, frozen, freezeAt, completeAt, nowMs)`: `nowMs ≥ freezeAt` and no binding freeze. `houseFreezeMonths(space, nowMs) → MonthId[]`: the UTC calendar month of `nowMs` and the one before, without months before the UTC month of `space.createdAt` (exactly the months the rules check).
- `agreeOutcome(space, subject, me, friends, stakes, house, nowMs) → { counts: 'now' | 'partly' | 'later'; waitingFor: UID[] }`: what my Agree to `subject`'s current terms would do (memberTerms with my agreement added at `nowMs`): `'now'` when both lines and the tiers would be on it, `'later'` when none would, else `'partly'`; `waitingFor` = the friends still needed for the rest. Example (a test): Marco's reward line is Same for everyone and his penalty line per friend, with streak rewards, and nobody has agreed; Lena's Agree is `'partly'`, waiting for Sam (her own penalty line counts at once; the reward line and the tiers wait for Sam), and Sam's Agree after hers is `'now'`.
- Debts: `Debt { monthId, subject, other, kind: 'reward' | 'penalty' | 'tier', what, together, tier?, source }`; `monthDebts(monthId, results, terms, tierRuns: Record<UID, Record<string, number>> | null)` (each reward or penalty debt takes `what`, `together` and `source` from its line; a tier debt for each friend whose terms carry tier `t`, when the subject passed and `tierRuns[subject][t.months] === t.months`, §9.8; tier debts are `together`); `DebtLine { subject, kind, what, tier?, together, source, debtors, creditors, keys: SettlementKey[] }`; `debtLines(debts)` (groups `together` debts with the same subject, kind, tier, text and source; ordered reward, tiers ascending, penalty); `pairSettlement(settlements, monthId, key)`; `lineSettlement(settlements, monthId, line) → { settled, last? }` (settled when every pair is).
- `ids.ts`: `settlementDocId(monthId, uid, other?, tier?)`.
- `firstMonthOf(space)` moves to `time.ts` (the screens keep their wrapper).
- Gone from the first draft: `agreementFrom`, `entryInForce`, `nextHistory` and an agreement's `from` month; a month's first lock plays no part in stakes any more.

### 9.8 Streak rewards and month results

**Rules of the game**
- A member's streak tiers are part of their stakes (agreed with them). After each complete month: passed → streak + 1; missed → 0; a month with no score (sat out, judged, every week paused) or with **nobody else playing** is skipped (no change). The streak is shown ("3 months in a row").
- Each streak reward counts **its own run, from when everyone agreed to it** (`tierRuns`, keyed by its length): in a month it is in force for the member (§9.7: the tiers everyone agreed to before the month was complete), a pass adds one and a skipped month carries; a miss resets every run to 0; a month it isn't in force drops its run (it starts again from 0 when it's back). A tier is owed **once per run**, in the month its run first equals its length, by every friend on those agreed terms, together; a pair on house stakes has no tiers. So adding a 3-month reward while on a 5-month streak needs 3 more months; one everyone agreed to during October counts October's result, but can't be won with September's.
- Results start with October 2026 (`FIRST_RESULTS_MONTH = '2026-10'`, the month batch 2 deploys in): earlier months have no results doc, streaks and runs start at 0 there, and no tier was ever in force before batch 2. The same month is the rules' literal `'2026-10'` (§9.7, `months/{monthId}`): from it on, the previous app's early freezes don't bind. Both must name the month the rules are published in; if the deploy slips past October's last Friday, change both to the deploy month first (otherwise a finished October that the previous app froze early would be re-frozen with whatever the space says by then).

**Data**
```
spaces/{id}/results/{monthId}   { monthId, completeAt: ts, members: { [uid]: { score: number|null, passed: bool|null, streak: int, tierRuns?: { [months]: int } } },
                                  terms?: { [subject]: { [other]: TermsInForce } }, createdAt: ts }
                                  TermsInForce = { reward: Line, penalty: Line, tiers: [{ months, reward }] },  Line = { text, together, source: 'agreed'|'house', at?: ts }
```
- Written once per month per space by the reminders job (admin `create()`), never updated (an admin deletes it to recompute). `members` = the month's roster plus everyone in the previous month's doc (streaks carry while someone sits out). `terms` = `monthTerms` for every player and friend of that month (≤ 56 pairs), from the agreements made before `completeAt` and the month's house stakes frozen at completion (the writer freezes them first when nobody has, or re-freezes the previous app's early freeze, §9.11); from then on they are that month's terms (§9.7). Clients only read (`match /results/{monthId}`: read by members; nothing else).

**Domain** (`stakes.ts`, `time.ts`)
- `FIRST_RESULTS_MONTH = '2026-10'`; `nextStreak(prev, month)`; `nextTierRuns(prev: Record<string, number> | undefined, month, tiersInForce: StreakTier[])` (Rules of the game); `buildMonthResults(monthId, completeAt, month, prev, terms)` (streaks, tier runs from `tiersInForce(terms, uid)` (its pairs with non-empty tiers, §9.7), and `terms`); `provisionalStreaks(monthId, firstMonthId, prev, month, terms) → { streaks, tierRuns } | null` (from the previous month's doc, or from 0 in the space's first month or in `FIRST_RESULTS_MONTH`; else null = unknown); `streaksFor(monthId, firstMonthId, own, prev, month, terms)` (the month's own doc, else provisional). Tier debts appear only when runs are known.
- `lastCompleteMonthCandidate(nowMs, tz)`: the latest month whose last week is before this week (deadlines are at most a week after Monday 00:00); the caller still checks the stored deadline.

**Data layer**: `useResults(space, monthId)` (key `${uid}|results|${spaceId}|${monthId}`), `useLatestResults(space)` (orderBy `monthId` desc, limit 1; key `${uid}|results|${spaceId}|latest`).

**Screens and copy**: month view hero pill "3 months in a row" (iridescent when a tier was reached this month, gray otherwise; shown when ≥ 2 and known); others' summary " · 3 in a row"; unknown for a complete month (from `FIRST_RESULTS_MONTH` on): "Streaks update within a day of the month ending.", or once it is more than 3 days past its completion "Streaks are running late. They’ll catch up." (the owner's sign that the reminders job needs a look, §9.14); nothing for months before `FIRST_RESULTS_MONTH`; reaching your own tier plays the existing verdict glow (no new moment).

### 9.11 Reminders

**Windows** (`scripts/reminders-plan.ts`). Each weekly window is defined on the week's own schedule (`week.schedule`, absent = usual, for a stored doc; the space's for a week without one) in nominal wall-clock minutes from its Monday, resolved with `momentAt` and closed by the **stored** bounds. Quiet hours: a warning that would land outside 08:00–22:00 moves back to 20:00 the evening before (`earlierAwake`); news that would land outside 09:00–21:00 waits for 09:00 (`laterAwake`; the verdict uses 10:00); event pushes are only sent 09:00–21:00 local. On top of that, a **send-time mute**: nothing at all is sent between 00:00 and 08:00 local (the run skips it with `not-yet` and the window stays open; with a deadline before 08:00, a last-call the evening before may not go out at all, which beats waking people). Windows are unchanged by it, so the legacy oracle still holds. With `L`, `R`, `D` the schedule's minutes:

| kind | opens | closes |
|---|---|---|
| plan | `earlierAwake(L − 360)` | stored lock |
| no-progress | 18:00 on the day halfway between lock and review opening; only if it opens after the lock and ends before open-tasks | midnight that day |
| open-tasks | `earlierAwake(R − 360)` (only if after the lock) | stored review opening |
| review | `max(reviewOpenAt, laterAwake(R))` (dropped when it would not open before last-call) | last-call's opening (expires at the deadline) |
| last-call | `max(reviewOpenAt, earlierAwake(D − 240))` | stored deadline |
| results | `max(deadlineAt, laterAwake(D))` | midnight two days after that day |

For the usual schedule this is exactly today's windows in every zone (a frozen copy of today's `dueWindows` is the test oracle, swept over 2 years in 8 zones at 30-minute steps). The job looks at **last, this and next** week (a Sunday-midnight deadline puts results on Monday; an early lock puts next week's plan push on Sunday). A paused week has no weekly windows, except that in a month's last week the monthly part of `review` still goes out (below). Existing kinds keep their keys (`${spaceId}_${weekId}_${kind}_${uid}`), so a deploy mid-window never sends twice.

**Verdict**: opens at `max(the 1st 10:00, laterAwake(completeAt, 10))`, closes at midnight two days after the opening day; `completeAt` = `monthEndsAt` (stored deadlines when loaded). Its decision is `not-yet` (not `final`) while `computeMonth` says the month isn't complete. It quotes **the terms that counted**: the month's results doc's `terms` (the results writer runs first in the same run), else `monthTerms` with `completeAt` and the month's house stakes (`monthHouse`: its freeze when it binds, else the space's). Copy per member M (`uniform` = all of M's debts of that kind have the same text): passed "You made it: 93%" · "October in Film Club is done. Collect: Dinner on me" (uniform) or "… Collect from Lena, Sam, and Kim." (mixed); with a tier reached "4 months in a row: 93%" · "… Collect: Dinner on me, plus Weekend away." / "… Collect from Lena and Sam, plus your streak reward."; missed "You fell short: 84%" · "… Pay up: Coffee for a week" / "… Pay up to Lena, Sam, and Kim." Tier text only when the month's results doc exists. Skips as today (no score, nobody else); judges-only have no score.

**Judges**: `plan` → `not-yet` (they can switch back before the lock; a doc's snapshot, else `spaceJudges`); `no-progress`, `open-tasks` → `final`; `review` as everyone (targets exclude judges); `last-call` for a judge: `hoursLeftTitle` · "Lena and Sam are waiting for your marks in Film Club." (no points); `results` for a judge: "Results are in" · "See how Film Club did this week." once revealed.

**Monthly lists**: in a month's last week, `review`, `last-call` and `results` also load that month's list (`DueWindow.monthIds`) and add its part. The monthly part has its own key `${spaceId}_${monthId}_${kind}_${uid}` next to the weekly `${spaceId}_${weekId}_${kind}_${uid}`: one push carries every part that is due and not yet logged, and logs the key of each part it carried; a decision is `final` only when both parts are. A missing week doc in a month's last week is treated like a paused week (monthly part only). When the stored monthly window differs from the week's by more than a minute (not healed yet), the monthly part is `not-yet` (the weekly part goes out alone; the monthly one follows under its own key once healed). Targets = the union of weekly and monthly targets still owed; `review` "Rate Anna and Ben in Film Club, October lists too. Due in 15 hours." (monthly part only, e.g. a paused week: "Rate the October lists of Anna and Ben in Film Club. Due in 15 hours."); `last-call` points = 5 × (weekly targets where you play) + 5 × (monthly targets where you're a monthly player, unless the week is paused), and no last-call when there are no points and you're no judge; `results` "You scored 85%, and 92% on your October list. See how Film Club did this week."

A non-owner's house-stakes edit from an old client is refused with the usual toast; an owner's still passes (without the notice), except between a month's completion and its freeze, when it is refused (the old app doesn't freeze at completion; any new client opening the space does). An old client's freeze of the current month at its first lock is refused (its upkeep ignores the refusal). An old client still shows October's early freeze where the previous app made one (with its "New stakes from November" footer) until it updates; new clients show the space's house stakes for October, which is what counts (§9.7). Old settlement writes (per member) still pass and still count.

### 9.15 Known limits

- A pause can be blocked by one member who never answers; the owner can remove them. The `pause-ask` push may arrive hours late or, near the review opening, not at all (the job runs every few hours and sends events only 09:00–21:00); the app only offers asking more than a day before reviews open, and the asker sees when it didn't happen.
- The 12-hour wait is per person and per their own last request: two members taking turns asking and saying Not Now can ask more often (each request still needs everyone's OK, and the job sends at most one `pause-ask` per request).
- Streaks and streak rewards depend on the reminders job writing month results. The app shows a provisional streak for one month (the previous results doc plus the month it has loaded); beyond that it says "Streaks update within a day of the month ending." (after three days, "Streaks are running late. They’ll catch up.").
- Friends can agree late in a month to soften someone's stakes once they see how it's going (an agreed change counts for the month in progress until it is complete). The owner accepted this; everyone sees the change on their stakes card and in the `stakes` push.
- A pair keeps its last 6 agreements, and only adds one when the terms changed. Finished months keep the terms stored in their results doc; a month without one (before `FIRST_RESULTS_MONTH`, or while the job is behind) is recomputed from the agreements, so after 6 more changes in that time its oldest agreements are gone and that month can fall back to the house stakes on screen.
- A friend who is in the space and hasn't agreed to someone's stakes keeps that member's later changes to a Same-for-everyone line (and to the streak rewards) from counting; what everyone agreed to before that friend joined keeps counting for the others, and the friend is on the house stakes. Per-friend lines aren't held up by them.
- Matching agreements to who was in the space uses the live `joinedAt`/`leftAt`: someone who left and rejoined counts from their latest join, so a month without a results doc can be recomputed differently after a rejoin.
- The rules insist that an owner's house-stakes edit finds every recently completed month (the current and previous UTC calendar month) frozen at completion, so nobody can change a finished month's house stakes in the minutes before someone freezes it. A month that completed longer ago and was never frozen (nobody opened the app since, and the reminders job is down) isn't checked; its results doc, once written, pins its terms.
- The house stakes are the owner's alone: the owner can change them right up to a month's completion and they count for it (as friends can agree late, above).
- The previous app froze October 2026 at its first lock. That freeze doesn't bind (§9.7), so the October house stakes are the space's until October completes and an owner's edit counts at once; an old client keeps showing the early freeze until it updates (§9.14).
- The stakes card and the month view of a month in progress wait for `termsFriends` (current members who aren't judges, and friends who left this month); the month's results wait for its `others`. They differ only for a member who played earlier in the month and judges now (the month view and the results wait for them; the card doesn't, and while judging they see no Agree) and a newcomer whose first week falls in the next month (the card waits for them; the results don't), so in those cases the card can change when the month completes.
- A month whose last week has no doc (nobody opened the app before its lock) and no monthly list ends at a time computed on the space's current schedule, so a later schedule change can move that month's `completeAt`, and with it which agreements count, until its results doc pins its terms (within a day).
- A month's house stakes can only be frozen within about two months (the rules' `request.time − 62d` window); an older month nobody froze (nobody opened the app after it ended, before batch 2's job wrote its results) shows the space's current house stakes.
- A monthly list's bounds may sit up to an hour off (as weeks may) until its lock week's doc exists; after that they equal the weeks' stored times.
- A schedule change made between a month's second week's new lock and its old lock, before the monthly list exists, leaves that month without a list (very rare).
- Spaces without `utcOffsetMin` (the job backfills it) can't have monthly lists.
- The two review submits of a month's last week aren't atomic; the sheet retries only the part that failed.
- A judge's ignored plan stays (switching back before the lock restores it).
- Stakes docs stay after `deleteMyAccount`, like plans and reviews.
- The rules can't know who judges when someone agrees to stakes or edits their own: only the UI hides Agree for judges.
- The `stakes` reminder costs one query read per space per job run during the space's daytime.
